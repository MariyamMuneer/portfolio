Nourish Lab

One component-based nutrition website, explored through three visual themes.

Role
Solo designer
Tools
Figma
Scope
Three desktop pages
Context
Internship exercise, 2026
01

Structure first.

I created navigation states, buttons, program cards and inputs as reusable components. The same three-page structure was restyled as Clinical Minimal, Modern Scientific and Vintage Apothecary.

The exercise explored how colour and typography can change the tone without changing the underlying content. Responsive layouts, additional form states and a more complete component library would be the next steps.
