Velora

An exploration of shopping navigation and comparison.

01

Shopping without search.

I explored finding airtight jars on Alibaba, Amazon and Daraz without using the search bar, recording navigation routes and product-review concerns. This was a one-person walkthrough, not a user study.

The resulting concept proposed guided categories, seller comparison and a shortlist. Two proto-personas were assumptions rather than interview findings. The screens were generated with AI tools and refined by me; the concept was not tested.

A good conversation is a good start.
